How scaffolding works

If you choose an unsafe or incomplete response, the module offers a simpler prerequisite question and a definition of the key concept. You can then return to the original decision. Later stages introduce new facts that may change the best response.

Completion reflects decisions and reflection, not professional certification. Emergency procedures and reporting duties must be checked against current local requirements and individual care plans.

OpenAI conversational extension (not active in this demo)

A production version could send the learner's de-identified response and scenario state to a server-side OpenAI integration. An instructor-approved rubric would evaluate reasoning, generate follow-up questions, and select scaffolding. The API key must stay on the server, and real child or family identifying details must not be transmitted.

This is an independent educational demonstration, not an official Kansas State University product. Purple and silver are used as a visual reference. Case details are anonymized and adapted from reported professional experiences; some answer options are instructional constructions.
